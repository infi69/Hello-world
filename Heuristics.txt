1.	smaller cros with low roi_h and roi_w are not decoded <100 and <100
2.	High quite zone scores are decode   >0.8-
3.	Low quite zone score with high lap_Score and edge_boder_ration is decoded
4.	Overall barcode scores should be above 0.70
5.	High barcode score with high roi_h and roi_w will be definiely decoded
6.	High quiet zone score is contributing in high barcode score
7.	If below metrics 
barcode_score >= 0.65
quiet_zone_score >= 0.12
glare_border_ratio <= 0.25
edge_density between 0.05 and 0.20
contrast >= 50
If these all pass, the crop is likely decodeable.
